# Storybook art direction

Branch: `design/enchanted-storybook`.

The attached sunset valley reference guided the palette, atmosphere, depth and rendering quality. The main menu, meadow and forest use new painted-style artwork; Paillette uses the new artwork throughout all seven worlds and in the reward scene. The other five habitat panoramas and animal companions retain their native illustrations. The classic fallback is unchanged.

Runtime artwork lives in `assets/storybook/`: `meadow.jpg` (menu vista), `meadow-game.jpg` (playfield with a low horizon), `forest.jpg`, and `paillette-0.webp` through `paillette-3.webp` (four transparent walk poses). Sources were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then encoded as optimized JPEGs and alpha-preserving WebPs. No image service or external font is required at runtime. The four poses were cropped from a 2×2 sheet without discarding alpha. The game retains its native path, bridge, checkpoint geometry, camera and interaction targets.

The menu uses an independent decorative vista while the hidden Phaser scene is paused. Starting an activity resumes the scene. Desktop places the menu beside Paillette; portrait phones place her above a scrollable activity panel. Reduced-motion behavior and large uppercase/lowercase answer cards remain in place.

## Generation prompts

### Menu vista

Use case: illustration-story. Create a production game background painting, 3:1 very wide panorama, preferably 2400 by 800 or larger. Inspired by the attached reference's enchanting sunlit fairytale valley, painterly 3D animated feature quality, lush and luminous, rich layered depth, blue lavender distant mountains, peach pink clouds, warm setting sun toward right, turquoise lake in middle distance, soft rolling green meadow with daisies, dappled golden light. Small ivory castle with elegant lilac blue spires on a distant hill on the far right. This is a BACKGROUND for a side scrolling children's unicorn game: NO unicorn, NO characters, NO animals, NO text, NO UI, NO path/road because a dynamic walkable path is drawn over it. Composition: horizon and mountains in upper 35%, rolling grassy meadow in lower 65%, open grassy foreground with no big obstacles. A few framing trees only at far edges. Sophisticated illustrated detail and soft atmospheric light, avoid flat vector shapes. Landscape must remain usable behind interactive game objects. Reference image is mood and visual quality guidance only.

### Paillette walk sheet (transparent)

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: transparent game character sprite sheet. Create a precisely aligned 2 by 2 grid of FOUR full body side view poses of the SAME adorable magical unicorn facing RIGHT. Each quadrant has equal size, with unicorn centered and same scale, feet on same baseline, generous clear transparent padding so no pose touches another. No grid lines, no labels, no ground, no shadows, transparent background. Character Paillette: pearl white softly shaded coat, flowing voluminous rose pink and lilac mane and tail with a few turquoise ribbons of hair, small spiral golden horn, dainty lavender hooves, expressive large violet eye with eyelashes, rosy cheeks, cheerful sweet smile. Feminine, funky and playful, beautiful polished animated feature 3D storybook rendering, golden rim light, luscious hair detail, real horse anatomy stylized into charming small pony proportions, not humanoid. Full body including horn tail and four legs visible and entirely within each cell. Four poses in reading order are successive WALK CYCLE key poses: (1) contact front near leg forward rear near leg back, (2) passing legs underneath body, (3) opposite contact front near leg back rear near leg forward, (4) opposite passing pose. Maintain head body height, silhouette scale and design exactly between poses. Strictly side-on camera so animation flows horizontally. No wings, no saddle, no clothing, no accessories, no text.

### Forest

Use case: illustration-story. Production side scrolling game background, very wide 3:1 landscape. Painterly polished animated feature enchanted forest, rich 3D detail, romantic pink lilac sunset light through towering old trees, luminous ferns, small flowers and glowing golden fireflies, distant turquoise woodland stream and a tiny tree house to the far right. Friendly inviting magical mood, no spooky darkness. Composition crucial: distant scenery in upper 35% only, lower 65% is a broad uninterrupted gently rolling mossy green meadow clearing, NO river water or obstacles in lower 65%, suitable for laying a dynamic horizontal walkable game path over it. Detailed grass and tiny flowers at very bottom. Light shafts, layered atmospheric trees, peach pink skies visible in gaps, beautifully rendered foliage. No characters, no unicorn, no animals, no text, no path, no UI. Harmonize with a fairytale sunlit meadow at golden hour and a pink/lilac unicorn.

### Meadow playfield

Use case: illustration-story. Create a landscape background for SIDE SCROLLING GAME. Extremely wide 3:1 panorama. CRITICAL COMPOSITION: horizon at 20% from TOP. Upper 20% a thin band of lavender mountains, tiny fairytale castle on far right, peach clouds and golden sunset sky. BOTTOM 80% a very broad open level GRASS MEADOW from edge to edge, with short softly textured lush grass, tiny pink flowers and daisies. NO water anywhere. NO cliffs. NO mountains in lower 80%. Grass must start no lower than 25% from top across the ENTIRE width. Camera looks gently downward on grassy playfield. This is a ground plane for a horizontal dynamic path to be drawn on top around 45% from top, therefore at that height there MUST be meadow grass for every horizontal position. Painterly cinematic 3D storybook rendering, detailed and softly glowing, light and shadow in grass, warm buttery gold and fresh sage green, soft pink sunset reflected in distant tree line. Very subtle small foliage in extreme bottom corners only, open central playfield, no objects, no road, no characters, no text, no UI. Match charming polished fairytale illustration rather than flat vector. Please prioritize the 80% grassy ground / 20% distant vista composition over conventional landscape composition.
